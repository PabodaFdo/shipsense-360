# ShipSense 360 Stakeholder Register

These roles are part of the fictional ShipSense 360 business scenario.

| Stakeholder | What they need | What they decide |
|---|---|---|
| Fulfilment manager | Late-shipping rates and risk scores for new orders | Which shipping modes to investigate and which orders the team should review first |
| Fulfilment coordinator | A list of orders flagged for review and the details needed to check them | Whether to follow up on an order or escalate it to the manager |
